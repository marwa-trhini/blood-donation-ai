
# Blood Donation AI

An AI-powered blood donation management system designed to connect patients in need of blood with eligible donors and assist with donor eligibility assessment.

## About the Project

Blood Donation AI is a web application developed as a Master's project to facilitate the process of finding suitable blood donors.

The system allows recipients to submit blood requests and helps identify potential donors based on blood type and eligibility criteria. An AI assistant is integrated into the platform to interact with donors, collect relevant health information, and assist in evaluating their eligibility for blood donation.

## Main Features

* Donor and recipient registration
* Blood donation requests
* Blood type-based donor matching
* Donor eligibility assessment
* AI-powered assistant for donors
* Communication between recipients and eligible donors
* Donor and recipient management
* Secure database management
* Responsive web interface

## AI Assistant

The AI assistant is designed to support the donation process by:

* Asking donors relevant health and eligibility questions
* Analyzing the provided information
* Providing an eligibility assessment
* Guiding donors through the donation process
* Helping recipients understand the next steps

> The AI assistant is intended as a decision-support feature and does not replace professional medical evaluation.
